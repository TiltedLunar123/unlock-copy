Always unlocked

Sites you chose to unlock on every visit.

    What this cannot do

    • Text drawn into a canvas, or shipped as an image, is not text. Nothing can copy it.
    • Text in CSS ::before or ::after content is not selectable by design.
    • Embedded frames from another site stay locked unless you unlock that site too.
    • The browser's built-in PDF viewer and browser pages are off limits to extensions.
